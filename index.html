Firearms & Weapons Violation (Article VI, Sec. 9) STRICT BAN

Possessing a firearm or deadly weapon inside any team or league facility is strictly prohibited:

  • Locker Room & Facility Ban: No player may possess a firearm or deadly weapon whenever physically present at a facility/venue owned, operated, or used by a Team or the NBA (including locker rooms, practice facilities, arenas, team offices, or while traveling on team business).
  • Conduct Prejudicial to League: Any breach is classified as conduct prejudicial to the NBA under Article 35(d) of the NBA Constitution.
  • Disciplinary Penalties: Triggers immediate disciplinary action by the Commissioner, including multi-game or indefinite suspensions without pay, substantial fines, and potential contract termination depending on severity.
  • Mandatory Annual Registration: Players owning licensed firearms must provide proof of legal registration and state licensing to the team annually.

First Apron Restrictions ($209.02M)

Crossing the 1st Apron hard-caps a team and triggers immediate roster restrictions:

  • No Sign-and-Trades: Teams cannot acquire a player via Sign-and-Trade if it keeps them above the 1st Apron.
  • Salary Matching Limit: Teams cannot take back more money in a trade than they send out (100% matching cap).
  • Buyout Market: Cannot sign players bought out during the regular season if their pre-buyout salary exceeded the Non-Taxpayer MLE ($15.04M).
  • No BAE: Access to the Bi-Annual Exception ($5.48M) is completely eliminated.

Second Apron Penalties ($221.69M) MAX RESTRICTION

Exceeding the 2nd Apron applies the strictest team-building penalties in modern NBA history:

  • No Aggregating Salaries: Teams cannot combine 2 or more player salaries to trade for a single higher-salaried player.
  • No Cash in Trades: Teams cannot send or receive cash considerations in any trade transaction.
  • No Mid-Level Exception: Access to all Mid-Level Exceptions (MLE) is lost. Teams can only offer Veteran Minimum contracts.
  • Frozen 1st-Round Draft Picks: First-round pick 7 years out is frozen (cannot be traded). If a team stays in the 2nd apron 2 out of 4 years, that pick drops to the 30th overall pick automatically.

Draft Pick Trade Rules

Rules governing how GMs can trade future draft assets:

  • THE STEPIEN RULE: A team cannot trade away its first-round pick in consecutive future drafts. (Teams must retain at least one 1st-round pick in every other future draft).
  • SEVEN-YEAR RULE: Teams can only trade draft picks up to 7 years in advance (e.g., in 2026, teams can trade picks up to 2033).
  • PICK SWAPS: Teams constrained by the Stepien Rule can still offer "Pick Swaps" to swap order with another team in consecutive years.

Mid-Level Exceptions (2026–27)

  • Non-Taxpayer MLE: $15.04M (Up to 4 years) — Hard caps team at 1st Apron.
  • Taxpayer MLE: $6.06M (Up to 2 years) — Available to tax teams under 2nd Apron.
  • Room Exception: $9.37M (Up to 3 years) — For teams below the salary cap.
